## BEST METHOD FOR TEAMS: Postman Cloud Workspace (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Go to Postman
https://www.postman.com → Sign In (or create account)

### Step 2: Open Postman Desktop App
1. Download from https://www.postman.com/downloads/
2. Sign in with your account
3. Open app

### Step 3: Import Collection
1. Click "Collections" (left sidebar)
2. Click "Import" button
3. Select: `School-Management-API.postman_collection.json` from your computer
4. Click "Import"

### Step 4: Create Workspace
1. Click "Workspaces" (top menu)
2. Click "Create Workspace"
3. Name: `School Management API`
4. Visibility: `Team` (to invite people)
5. Click "Create"

### Step 5: Move Collection to Workspace
1. Right-click collection you imported
2. Click "Move to Workspace"
3. Select: `School Management API`
4. Done!

### Step 6: Invite Team Members
1. Click workspace name (top right)
2. Click "Members" or "Settings"
3. Click "Invite Users"
4. Enter team member emails
5. Click "Send Invites"

### Step 7: Share Workspace Link
Team members will receive email invitation with workspace link

**Result**: Everyone has access in their Postman accounts!
