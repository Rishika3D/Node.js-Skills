## 📌 Option 3: Share via Postman Cloud

### Create Postman Workspace (Recommended for Teams)

#### Step 1: Sign in to Postman

1. Go to: https://www.postman.com
2. Click "Sign In" or "Sign Up"
3. Create account if needed

#### Step 2: Import Collection in Postman

1. Download Postman Desktop App
2. Click "Collections" (left sidebar)
3. Click "Import"
4. Select: `School-Management-API.postman_collection.json`
5. Click "Import"

#### Step 3: Create Workspace

1. Click "Workspaces" (top menu)
2. Click "Create Workspace"
3. Name: "School Management API"
4. Visibility: "Team" (if sharing)
5. Click "Create"

#### Step 4: Move Collection to Workspace

1. Right-click collection
2. Select "Move to Workspace"
3. Choose: "School Management API"
4. Done!

#### Step 5: Share Workspace

1. Click workspace settings (top right)
2. Click "Members"
3. Click "Invite Users"
4. Enter team emails
5. Send invites

**Team members get**: Direct access in their Postman accounts
